verdict

today the near token captures a deposit, not a toll: near a year locked under state and burned as gas, against issued.

the case for

  • the lock is real and protocol-enforced: near under one contract, readable with one rpc call.
  • it grew at every one of checkpoints across the year, from to .
  • it scales with stored state, which is what agents and accounts produce, not with gas price.
  • fee vaults already hold near that governance could burn.

the case against

  • it is too small: of net issuance.
  • it is locked, not burned. deleting state returns it.
  • it is paid by a treasury: of top-ups in days came from one funded account.
  • the fee vaults are balances, not burns. the buyback vault moves on of keys.
  • this measures one contract. it is not a census of chain state.

what would change my mind

  • state growth near gb a year instead of ; a tenth of that would already matter.
  • the refills stop and the lock keeps growing: someone other than the treasury is posting collateral.
  • vault balances are burned and total supply falls by the same amount.
  • any of the three tests below fails on a rebuild.
